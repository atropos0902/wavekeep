# Why 90%

The floor decides when an engine is left out of a comparison. With no floor, the answers that survive a bad day stand in for the ones that didn't, and those differ: long answers time out more and name more brands. With a floor of 100% one lost capture throws away an engine.

90% is a judgement. At 90% the missing tenth can move a rate by at most 10 points if every missing answer went one way, and by far less in practice. The bounds printed beside the rate show the worst case, so nobody has to take the floor on trust. Moving it is one constant, and `bench/false-change.ts` is what to run after.
