# Workspaces, roles and reports

**Who asked.** An agency lead who wants a person to read every report before a client does.

**What they'll do with it.** Draft, review, approve, share. A client account sees approved reports and no other screen.

**What's left out.** Scheduled sending, white-label themes, comments.

**The check.** An approved report's bytes are hashed when it's approved, and the shared page refuses bytes that don't match. Fifty later changes to names and detectors leave them identical.
