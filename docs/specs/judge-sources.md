# Recommendation judge and sources

**Who asked.** A client who was named often and recommended rarely, and couldn't see that from one score.

**What they'll do with it.** Read the three numbers separately, and see which pages the answers came from.

**What's left out.** A combined score.

**The check.** The rule judge is tested against planted instructions. The model judge needs 300 answers labeled by two people before it's switched on, and nobody has labeled them yet, so it's off.
