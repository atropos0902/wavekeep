# Waves and compare

**Who asked.** The same analyst, after a report said the client gained nine points in a week when one engine had been down.

**What they'll do with it.** Tell a client what changed, or tell them plainly that this wave can't say.

**What's left out.** A pooled rate subtracted from another pooled rate, in any form. The compare screen prints that subtraction once, in a sentence that says why it isn't a result.

**The check.** Under 6% of unchanged brands are reported as changed, in four situations. At least half of the real lifts of 0.6 log-odds are found when every engine is up.
